# MarioMind

MarioMind is a reproducible reinforcement-learning lab for controlled experiments with agents in a Gym-compatible World 1-1 environment.

## What I built
- DQN and Double DQN agents with replay buffers and target networks.
- Configurable frame stacking, reward shaping, exploration schedules, replay sizes, and action spaces.
- Random, always-right, and reflex baselines for experiment sanity checks.
- Evaluation, plotting, and failure-summary tooling for comparing runs.
- A classical-search toy environment for comparing model-based planning with reinforcement learning.

## Technical focus
Python, PyTorch, Gym/Gymnasium, deep Q-learning, experiment design, ablation studies, and failure analysis.
