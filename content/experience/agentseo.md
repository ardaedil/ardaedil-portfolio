# AgentSEO - Founder

## Overview
I am building AgentSEO as my startup. The product measures how reliably AI models operate an API before they are trusted with production workflows.

Repository: https://github.com/ardaedil/AgentSEO

## What I built
- A working research MVP for the question of how interface design affects AI-agent reliability.
- OpenAPI 3.x import, normalized tool definitions, and generated task catalogs.
- Resettable synthetic API sandboxes for e-commerce, SaaS billing, and CRM workflows.
- Bounded MockAgent, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini function-calling adapters.
- Deterministic state assertions, raw metrics, experimental scoring, failure taxonomy, and visible event traces.
- A FastAPI, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, PostgreSQL, Next.js, TypeScript, React, and Tailwind product stack.

## What this demonstrates
- Startup ownership and product direction
- AI-agent evaluation and tool-use reliability
- Backend/API design and full-stack engineering
- Experiment design, observability, testing, Docker, and CI
